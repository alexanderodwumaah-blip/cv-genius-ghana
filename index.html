    Learn to Write a Powerful CV

    Master the art of professional CV writing with these guidelines, curated for the Ghanaian and African job market.

    01

    CV Length

    Keep your CV to 1 page if you have under 3 years of experience. You can achieve this by being concise, removing unnecessary data, and adjusting font size and margins.

    02

    Font Choice

    Use a formal font — Times New Roman is highly recommended. Font size should be between 9.5–11 for body text, and up to size 22 for your name only.

    03

    Font Colour

    Use black font only. Coloured text can appear unprofessional and may not print well on standard printers.

    04

    File Format

    Always submit your CV as a PDF document. Never send an MS Word document — formatting can shift on different computers.

    01

    Your Name

    Write in the order: First Name, Middle Name (optional), Last Name. Do not put your family name first.

    02

    Contact Info

    Include your professional email address and phone number with its country code (e.g. +233). Keep it clean and easily readable.

    03

    LinkedIn Profile

    Having a LinkedIn account and linking it in your CV gives you more credibility with recruiters. Ensure your profile is up to date.

    04

    Education

    Highlight only your tertiary education. Include university name, location, programme, academic standing, and relevant courses. No secondary school.

    05

    Work Experience

    Include: organisation name, your title, location, time period, and your achievements — not just responsibilities. Show impact with data.

    06

    Skills Section

    List only skills relevant to the role you are applying for. Tailor this section for every application.

    01

    Use Action Words

    Start every bullet with a strong verb: Developed, Built, Led, Executed, Increased, Analysed, Designed, Managed. Never say "Responsible for..."

    02

    Quantify Your Impact

    Use real figures wherever possible: "Reduced processing time by 25%", "Managed a portfolio of GHS 500,000", "Onboarded 5 new enterprise clients".

    03

    Tailor to the Role

    Highlight work experience and certifications most relevant to the job. If applying for finance, lead with finance skills — don't bury them.

    04

    Write Full Names

    Write organisation names in full: "Goldman Sachs International", not "GS". Not all recruiters know every acronym.

    05

    Avoid Jargon Overload

    Don't overuse highly technical jargon or acronyms that a recruiter (who may not be technical) would not understand.

    06

    Portfolio / GitHub

    If your field requires a portfolio (engineering, design, software), include a short, functional link. Use a link shortener like Bitly if needed.

    01

    Date Consistency

    Use only one date format throughout your CV. Don't mix "Jul 2023" with "March 2022". Pick one style and stick to it.

    02

    Formatting Consistency

    Be consistent with bold vs normal text, capitalisation, bullet point types, hyphen lengths, and spacing. Visual consistency signals attention to detail.

    03

    Grammar & Spelling

    Proofread multiple times. Stick to either British or American English consistently — don't mix them. Spelling errors are red flags.

    04

    Check All Links

    Ensure every link in your CV is functional and accessible. A broken LinkedIn link is worse than no link at all.

    05

    Name Your File

    Name your CV file professionally: "Veronica Mensah – CV.pdf". Not "CV_final_v3_REAL.pdf".

    06

    Get a Second Opinion

    Proofread your CV multiple times — both individually and with others. Fresh eyes catch what yours miss.

    ✗

    Personal Data

    Do NOT include: gender, age, date of birth, marital status, residential address, religion, nationality, or race/ethnicity.

    ✗

    Headshot / Photo

    Do NOT include a headshot or photo unless specifically requested. It's not standard for professional applications.

    ✗

    Beginner Languages

    Do NOT list languages you only speak at beginner level. Only include languages you are at intermediate level or above.

    ✗

    Secondary School

    Do NOT include your secondary/high school education unless the application process specifically requests it.

    ✗

    Unprofessional Email

    Do NOT use casual email addresses like sexygirl54@gmail.com. Your email should be a professional variation of your name.

    ✗

    References

    References are generally not needed on a CV. "References available upon request" is also unnecessary — it's assumed.

    💪 Power Action Verbs for Your CV

    Start every bullet point with a strong action verb. Here are the best verbs organised by category:

    Leadership & Management

    Led · Managed · Oversaw · Directed · Coordinated · Supervised · Spearheaded · Championed · Delegated · Mentored · Guided · Established

    Analysis & Research

    Analysed · Researched · Evaluated · Assessed · Examined · Investigated · Reviewed · Identified · Diagnosed · Forecasted · Modelled · Interpreted

    Creation & Development

    Developed · Designed · Built · Created · Launched · Implemented · Engineered · Constructed · Drafted · Authored · Produced · Programmed

    Achievement & Improvement

    Increased · Reduced · Improved · Achieved · Delivered · Exceeded · Optimised · Streamlined · Accelerated · Generated · Maximised · Saved

    Collaboration & Communication

    Collaborated · Negotiated · Presented · Communicated · Facilitated · Trained · Advised · Liaised · Partnered · Supported · Advocated · Pitched

    Finance & Operations

    Executed · Managed · Reconciled · Audited · Processed · Allocated · Budgeted · Forecasted · Reported · Administered · Compiled · Monitored

    ❌ Never use these: "Responsible for..." · "Was on..." · "Helped with..." · "Worked on..." · "Was involved in..." · "Duties included..."

    🇬🇭

    National Service

    Emphasise your academic standing, leadership roles, community involvement, and extracurriculars. GPA and class of degree are important. Keep to 1 page.

    🏦

    Banking & Finance

    Quantify everything — deal values, portfolio sizes, cedis managed. Highlight FMVA, CFA, ACCA certifications. Commercial awareness and analytical skills are key.

    💻

    Tech & Engineering

    Lead with your technical skills section. Include GitHub, specific languages and tools, project work, and any engineering certifications. Quantify system improvements.

    🔬

    Academic / Research

    Lead with GPA and class of degree. Include publications, research projects, conference presentations, and academic awards. Use an academic CV format if needed.

    🎓

    Postgraduate Studies

    Foreground your academic excellence and research interests. Include your final year project or dissertation topic. Show evidence of independent thinking and scholarship.

    🎯

    Graduate Programmes

    Highlight leadership, commercial awareness, internship experiences, and any measurable impact. Graduate recruiters love initiative, quantified results, and well-rounded profiles.

    🌍

    NGO / Development

    Emphasise community impact, volunteer experience, and data that demonstrates social change. Show your commitment to the mission. Include languages and field experience.

    📋

    Internships

    Keep to 1 page. Lead with your strongest academic achievement and any prior work experience. Include relevant courses and skills. Show enthusiasm and eagerness to learn.
